# Business Headlines

Headlines answers: what did Loop observe, how do the signals connect, what could that mean for this business, and what remains unestablished? It is separate from daily obligations and the measured objective signals. No connection is preferable to a fabricated one. An INSIGHT cites at least two distinct artifacts, including internal evidence, and uses conditional wording; numeric, date, reference and causal-overreach validation remain. CallGrid plus its campaign breakdown is one underlying event stream, not independent corroboration.

## Evidence and scope

Current authorized Mail, Chats, Calendar, CallGrid, Campaigns, Creator Hub, websites, CRM, pipeline and Work readings can contribute. A domain must actually have current data; connection badges do not supply it. The signed-in person's private readings remain private. The chat scan is bounded to 100 digests; context retains a domain's first reading before additional detail, at most 24 artifacts, then fits the existing token allowance. Missing, stale or omitted evidence is a gap, never quiet.

Public news initially covers the FTC press-release feed, Google Ads & Commerce, YouTube, Digiday and Search Engine Land. Their HTTPS RSS endpoints and the production parser were exercised on October 16: 10 FTC, 4 Google Ads, 2 YouTube, 15 Digiday and 10 Search Engine Land articles within the 14-day ingestion window. This is external-source plumbing validation, not production database ingestion. No article bodies or private LinkedIn feeds are read. Only title, URL, publisher and publication date are retained. No private search query or credentials go to publishers. The model receives up to three locally relevant titles published within seven days, at most one per publisher; relevance is lexical overlap with authorized internal evidence, not a determination of business impact. Every used article is attributed with its date and original link. Applicability and article-body verification remain unknown. More sources and an authorized LinkedIn data integration remain follow-up work, not secretly available through ChatGPT's connected plugins.

Feeds refresh in the existing commissioned briefing worker, at most every 30 minutes per organization/feed, under a revalidated configured acting operator. Fixed URLs/hosts, no redirects, 12-second request timeout, one-megabyte streamed body cap, DTD/entity rejection, publication-date validation and metadata minimization. Publication time is never replaced by fetch time. Feed failure excludes its articles from current synthesis; two-hour health expiry applies. Retention is 30 days from publication; the worker purges expired rows for the organization. No historical learning/training claim is made.

Campaign growth priorities require recent observed activity when event time is available. Literal provider NO_ROUTE statuses are counted separately from provider-asserted noRoute booleans; the API can omit the latter. All-no-route attempts cannot become connected-call growth or evidence of incurred cost. Old records remain in weekly financial totals, and distinct provider campaign IDs remain distinct. Missing outcome flags carry coverage rather than becoming false.
